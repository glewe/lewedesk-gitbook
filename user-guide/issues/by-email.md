# Issues by Email

If your administrator switched on [Email to Ticket](../../administration/system/email-to-ticket.md), you can also work with issues from your mail program.

## Create an issue

Write a mail to the support address, **from the address of your LeweDesk account**. The subject becomes the summary, the text the description. Pictures in the text and attached files (txt, md, pdf, zip, gif, jpg, png) are added to the issue. You get a mail with the key of the new issue. The issue is visible to you and to the support team unless the administrator chose a wider access.

{% hint style="info" %}
A mail from an address that is not the address of an account is not accepted, unless your administrator allows it.
{% endhint %}

## Answer an issue

The mails about your issues carry the line

> Please type your reply above this line

Answer such a mail and write **above** the line. Your text becomes a comment on the issue; the quoted history of the mail and your signature are cut off. You can also write a new mail with the key of the issue (for example _LD-123_) in the subject.

* Comments by mail are always public to everybody who can see the issue; there are no internal comments by mail.
* An answer does not change the status of the issue.
* Attached files are checked like uploads in the browser. If one is not accepted, the comment is added and says which file was left out.
* An issue that is closed takes your comment, but no attachments.

In the [issue list](list-issues.md), issues made from a mail show an envelope next to the key.
